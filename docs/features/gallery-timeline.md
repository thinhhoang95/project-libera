# Browsing the gallery by date

The gallery's toolbar groups photos by date, jumps to a date, and filters photos by who added them and when. A seeker beside the photos scrolls through years of photos at once. These controls appear in Photos, Starred, the Protected Archive and every album.

## Group by

**Group by** (the calendar button, showing the current choice) splits the photos into sections:

- **Auto** starts a new section where the dates jump: a gap much longer than the gaps between the photos around it (an outing, a trip, an afternoon of generating), a gap of over a week, or a new month after a pause of a few hours. A section is labelled with the day, or the days, it covers. Sections on the same day also show the time they start.
- **Day**, **Week** (Monday to Sunday), **Month** and **Year** follow the calendar.

The choice is remembered on this computer. The tick beside a section's label still selects all its photos.

## Jump to

**Jump to** scrolls to the photo taken closest to a day. Pick a day, or click one of the years and months listed under it: only those with photos are listed. The photo it lands on is outlined for a moment.

## Filter

**Filter** shows only some of the photos:

- **Added by** lists everyone who added photos to the view, with how many. **You** is your own photos, including those you added to shared albums. Tick one or more people to show only their photos. Photos only you have show no list.
- **Taken** limits the photos to a range of days. Either end can stay open.

While a filter is on, the button shows how many parts are set, the header counts "*n* of *total* photos", and a bar under the toolbar says what is filtered, with **Clear**. Selecting all (⌘A / Ctrl+A), the viewer's previous and next, and Jump to work on the filtered photos. The filter stays while you move between views and is cleared when the app restarts.

## The seeker

The rail on the right of the photos replaces the scroll bar. Years are marked where their photos start. Hover over it to see the month at that point, and click or drag to go there. The thick mark shows the part of the gallery in view. With the rail focused, the arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End scroll too.

The seeker shows when there is more than about one and a half screens of photos.

## Command palette

With the gallery open, the command palette (⌘/Ctrl+Shift+P) lists the toolbar's buttons and these commands too:

- **Group by auto**, **day**, **week**, **month** and **year**, with the current one marked.
- **Jump to newest photo**, **Jump to oldest photo**, and **Jump to** each year with photos.
- **Filter: added by …** for each contributor (run it again to untick), **Filter: taken in the last 30 days**, **in the last 12 months**, and **in** each year, and **Clear filter**.

In the photo viewer, it adds **Previous photo**, **Next photo**, **First photo**, **Last photo**, **Fit to screen**, **Actual size**, and the Info panel's **Reuse prompt**, **Use as reference** and **Revert to original**.
