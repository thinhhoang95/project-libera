Search

Global Search finds notes, PDFs, LaTeX, code and images by meaning with Google’s EmbeddingGemma 2, running on this computer. Keyword search works without it. Changes apply without restarting.

EmbeddingGemma 2 weights

Download the ONNX model from Hugging Face (onnx-community/embeddinggemma-2-ONNX): config.json, tokenizer.json, tokenizer_config.json, processor_config.json and preprocessor_config.json, plus the weight files below, each with its .onnx_data file. Keep the weights outside Libera; choose them here. On a CPU, the 8-bit files (model_quantized.onnx) are as fast as the 4-bit ones and closer to the original.

A model_*.onnx file. config.json and tokenizer.json must be in its folder or the folder above.

A vision_encoder_*.onnx file. Needed to index photos and to search with images; leave empty for text only.
