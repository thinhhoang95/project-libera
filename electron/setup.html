About prompt caching

Enabled routes only to caching-capable providers and adds explicit cache breakpoints where supported, including Gemini. Cache writes may cost extra and expire; repeated context is needed for savings. Disabled removes cache breakpoints and routing restrictions, but providers may still cache automatically. With caching on, routing still applies among the caching providers.

Search

Global Search finds notes, PDFs, LaTeX, code and images by meaning with Google’s EmbeddingGemma 2, running on this computer. Keyword search works without it. Changes apply without restarting.

Models on this computer

EmbeddingGemma 2

Understands what notes and photos are about. Leave both fields empty to use Libera’s download.

A model_*.onnx file. config.json and tokenizer.json must be in its folder or the folder above.

A vision_encoder_*.onnx file. Needed to index photos and to search with images; with your own text model, leave empty for text only.

Using your own weights

Leave both empty and Libera offers to download the 8-bit text model and vision encoder (about 540 MB) from the Search panel, into its application data. To use other weights, download the ONNX model from Hugging Face (onnx-community/embeddinggemma-2-ONNX): config.json, tokenizer.json, tokenizer_config.json, processor_config.json and preprocessor_config.json, plus the weight files above, each with its .onnx_data file, and choose them here. On a CPU, the 8-bit files (model_quantized.onnx) are as fast as the 4-bit ones and closer to the original.

PDF reading model

IBM’s granite-docling-258M (Apache-2.0) reads pages for Snip as Markdown and Convert PDF to Markdown.

What goes in this folder

The first time you use either feature, Libera offers to download it (about 600 MB) into this folder, or into its application data when the field is empty. Or put the files from Hugging Face (onnx-community/granite-docling-258M-ONNX) in a folder yourself, laid out like the repository: the configs and tokenizer at the top, and vision_encoder, embed_tokens and decoder_model_merged weights in onnx/.

Speech

Turns speech into text: voice memos get timestamped transcripts that tell speakers apart, and the microphone in AI Chat, the Agent, Agentic Review, Write with AI and AI Rewrite types what you say. Changes apply without restarting.

On this computer

Speech models and the audio converter that voice memos and dictation share.

What goes in this folder

The first time you transcribe, Libera offers to download them here, or into its application data when the field is empty: NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (sherpa-onnx 8-bit export, about 670 MB), and pyannote segmentation-3.0 with 3D-Speaker CAM++ to tell speakers apart (34 MB together). Or put the files there yourself, in parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3/ (encoder, decoder and joiner .onnx files and tokens.txt), pyannote-segmentation-3.0/model.onnx and speaker-embedding/*.onnx. Voice memos and dictation share them.

Reads M4A, MP3, OGG, FLAC and other recording formats. Without it, macOS reads them with its own converter and other systems read WAV only. Dictation does not need it.

Voice memos

Transcription

Timestamped transcripts of your recordings.

Engine and speed

Kokoro runs on the CPU and nothing leaves the computer. OpenRouter receives the text a few sentences at a time and bills per character.

Kokoro on this computer always follows the speed; on OpenRouter only some providers do.

Kokoro 82M on this computer

Voices for kokoro-multi-lang-v1_0, grouped by accent.

Other releases fall back to their first voice for names they do not have.

Using another release

Leave the folder empty and the first time you read aloud, Libera offers to download kokoro-int8-multi-lang-v1_0 (about 190 MB) into its application data. To use another release, such as the full-precision kokoro-multi-lang-v1_0 or the English-only kokoro-en-v0_19, download it from sherpa-onnx’s tts-models release on GitHub, extract it, and choose the folder holding model.onnx, voices.bin, tokens.txt and espeak-ng-data/.

OpenRouter

hexgrad/kokoro-82m (the default) costs well under a dollar per million characters and has the same voices as Kokoro on this computer. Others, such as openai/gpt-4o-mini-tts or google/gemini-3.8-flash-lite-tts, sound more expressive for more.

Each model has its own voices; the list suggests the chosen model’s. Empty uses the provider’s default, where it has one.

Detection and Segmentation

Finds, outlines and counts objects in photos with Ultralytics’ YOLOE-26, on this computer. Describe what to find in words, or draw examples on the photo or on a reference image. Open it with the scan button in the photo viewer and the Image Gallery. Changes apply to the next run, without restarting.

Model

The prompt models for text and visual prompts. The tool can switch variants for a run; each downloads the first time it is used.

mAP50-95 on LVIS minival, from Ultralytics.

The CPU works everywhere and takes well under a second per photo for the smaller variants. The GPUs are faster for the larger variants, where PyTorch supports them.

Inference

How detections are kept and how closely photos are examined.

Detections scoring below it are dropped (default 0.25). The tool can raise it afterwards without running again.

How much two boxes may overlap before the weaker is suppressed (default 0.7).

The photo’s longer side is resized to the inference size before detection; larger sizes find smaller objects such as cells or seeds.

Downloads

A private Python runtime and the model weights, kept in one folder.

The first time you detect, Libera offers to download a private Python runtime with PyTorch and Ultralytics (about 1 GB, installed by uv into this folder, nothing system-wide), MobileCLIP2-B for text prompts (254 MB) and the chosen variant’s weights from Ultralytics’ GitHub releases.
